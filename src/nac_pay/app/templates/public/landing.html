Know what you earned.

NAC Pay Tracker is a private pay-tracking tool for airline pilots. It reads the monthly schedule documents you already receive, computes your credited pay hours under your contract's pay rules, and flags days where the company's published figures don't match.

What it does

  • Builds a calendar of your month from your own schedule documents.
  • Recomputes each day's credit — trip pay, duty rigs, guarantees, premiums.
  • Highlights discrepancies so you can file for the pay you're owed.

Access

This is a private tool, not a public service. Creating an account requires details from your airline crew documents, so signup is effectively limited to invited crewmembers.
