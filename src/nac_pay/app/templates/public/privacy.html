Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

What we collect

  • Account details — your email address and a password (stored only as a salted hash, never in plain text).
  • Schedule documents — the crew schedule files you upload or connect, used solely to compute your pay figures.
  • A session cookie — keeps you signed in. We set no advertising or tracking cookies.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not run ads or third-party analytics on your data.
  • We do not send marketing email — only account email you request (verification, password reset).

Where your data lives

Your data is stored on our own server, isolated per account, and is visible only to you when signed in.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and every document you've uploaded at any time from your account settings, or by emailing support@pch-ledger.com.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@pch-ledger.com.
